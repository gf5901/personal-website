## My Background

My parents were impoverished immigrants from China. My dad, following his brothers' footsteps, started a Chinese restaurant in the huge metropolis of Rigby, Idaho. This town is also where the inventor of the television, Philo Farnsworth, grew up. My little brother and I used to wait and bus tables, wash dishes, and prep food. Sadly, even with two chefs for parents, I have no culinary skills, so for me there was no future of running the family business.

Fortunately for me, I was able to learn how to program when I was an early teen. I grew up with a Windows 95 computer and then a Windows XP.  I had no internet, so I had to go to the public library to look up how to do things during my 1 hour sessions. Some of my favorite pastimes included modding games like Warcraft 3 and Little Fighter 2. The library sold floppy disks which used to download code files onto to bring home to try out.

When I was in high school, I originally wanted to study neurochemistry and become a neuroscientist. Chemistry was one of my favorite subjects in school. But one day I received a brochure from a school called Neumont University. They knew how to market to their target audience, because I really liked the design of the brochure. I could get a degree in 2.5 years with 97% guarantee of a high paying job upon graduation. I decided to attend this school instead of the other more well known schools I was accepted into. Ever since I have had a long career at startups and tech companies building things like software for public safety, private security, aerospace defense, and much more.

As a teenager, I was misdiagnosed with iron deficiency anemia. I don't blame my doctor, as medical information was not as available back then. Ten years later, I learned that my actual condition is beta thalessemia, a genetic hemoglobin mutation that makes my blood cells very small causing constant lethargy (I was never good at PE). This combined with my early obsession/fear of death and interest in human biology put me onto a path of health research. I'm successfully maintaining my condition now, but I realize that everyone could face similar issues and better access to health information can be life changing. There are no words to describe how grateful I am to be able to work with Bryan Johnson to help others become their best selves (and also survive our impending AIpocalypse).

## Philosophy

I feel pretty lucky that I am naturally gifted with certain skills. I've been able to learn a lot quickly despite how lazy I am. There is not a single day that I take for granted. I love life, and I love to build things that help others feel the same way.

I am pretty unconventional. I believe there is no reason for anyone to be ordinary. The world is ripe with interesting ways to change and improve to our liking.
